Independent Advice, with Nothing to Sell

I do not sell security guards, alarms, cameras, or any other products or equipment. I only provide my services, so my training is never tied to something I am selling. My only interest is giving you honest advice and the best client service.

How I Help Retail Spaces

Emergency Response Planning

  • I audit, test, and review your loss prevention, robbery response, and life-safety procedures.
  • I develop plans for threats, irate customers, robberies, and crowd control.
  • I lead employee training in emergency response and store protocols.

Security Director on Demand

  • High-level security consulting without the overhead of a full-time employee.
  • I act as your dedicated liaison with law enforcement and the Mid-Atlantic Organized Retail Crime Alliance to combat organized retail crime.
  • I conduct independent investigations of theft, fraud, and employee misconduct.

Physical Security Assessment

  • An independent audit of your storefronts, point-of-sale areas, inventory rooms, loading docks, and offices.
  • I identify vulnerabilities to external theft, internal and external shrink, and physical safety threats.

Background Screening

  • Thorough background screening of employees and contractors.
  • I use proprietary databases that are available only to licensed firms, and I analyze the results myself as a former law enforcement officer to find red flags that automatic and instant background check services miss.
