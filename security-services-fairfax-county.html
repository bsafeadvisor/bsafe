Security in Fairfax County

Fairfax County is Virginia's most populous jurisdiction. It is home to major corporate offices, professional services firms, federal contractors, large school systems, and busy retail and restaurant districts such as Tysons and Reston Town Center.

Organizations here often handle sensitive information, employ many people, and welcome the public every day. Their risks include workplace violence, employee theft and fraud, and after-hours access. Families face their own concerns, including harassment, threats, and security at home.

Fairfax County at a Glance

  • Fairfax County was formed in 1742 from the northern part of Prince William County and is named for Thomas Fairfax, 6th Lord Fairfax of Cameron. Source for this fact about Fairfax County
  • The 2020 Census counted 1,150,309 residents, making Fairfax the most populous county in Virginia. Source for this fact about Fairfax County
  • The county covers about 391 square miles of land and is governed by a 10-member Board of Supervisors. Source for this fact about Fairfax County
  • The county seat is the independent City of Fairfax, which sits within the county but is not part of it. Source for this fact about Fairfax County
  • Fairfax County has three incorporated towns, Clifton, Herndon, and Vienna, plus larger communities such as Reston, McLean, Tysons, Centreville, and Springfield. Source for this fact about Fairfax County

Towns, Cities, and Communities in Fairfax County

I serve every town and city in and around Fairfax County, including:
