Security in Fairfax County

Fairfax County is Virginia's most populous jurisdiction. It is home to major corporate offices, professional services firms, federal contractors, large school systems, and busy retail and restaurant districts such as Tysons and Reston Town Center.

Organizations here often handle sensitive information, employ many people, and welcome the public every day. Their risks include workplace violence, employee theft and fraud, and after-hours access. Families face their own concerns, including harassment, threats, and security at home.
