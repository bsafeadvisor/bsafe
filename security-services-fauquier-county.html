Security in Fauquier County

Fauquier County blends small towns with farms, horse properties, wineries, and event venues. Security questions here are different from those in a city. Properties can be large and isolated, neighbors may be far away, and travel times for responders can be longer in rural areas.

Those conditions make planning matter even more. A clear emergency plan, a review of access to your property, and a trusted advisor you can call quickly help owners of businesses, venues, farms, and homes decide what to do before something happens.

Fauquier County at a Glance

  • Warrenton is the county seat of Fauquier County. Source for this fact about Fauquier County
  • The 2020 census counted 72,972 residents in Fauquier County. Source for this fact about Fauquier County
  • The county covers about 651 square miles, nearly all of it land. Source for this fact about Fauquier County
  • Fauquier County was created in 1759 from part of Prince William County and named for Lieutenant Governor Francis Fauquier. Source for this fact about Fauquier County
  • Fauquier borders Clarke, Loudoun, Prince William, Stafford, Culpeper, Rappahannock, and Warren counties. Source for this fact about Fauquier County
