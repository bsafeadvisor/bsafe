Security in Prince William County

Prince William County is one of the largest and most diverse communities in Virginia. Busy commuter corridors along I-95 and I-66, large shopping centers, and Marine Corps Base Quantico on its southern edge shape daily life here.

Retail corridors and large shopping centers can make retail theft and organized retail crime a real concern for local businesses. I am a member of the Mid-Atlantic Organized Retail Crime Alliance, and I help owners protect inventory, train staff, and investigate losses. Schools, houses of worship, and families have their own needs, from entry control to threat assessment.

Prince William County at a Glance

  • The Prince William County Fire and Rescue System combines the county Department of Fire and Rescue with eight volunteer fire and rescue departments to serve the county and its incorporated towns. Source for this fact about Prince William County
  • The county's Fire and Rescue System describes itself as protecting and serving Prince William County since 1938. Source for this fact about Prince William County
  • The county lists 22 fire stations, run by both the county Department of Fire and Rescue and volunteer departments. Source for this fact about Prince William County
  • The Department of Fire and Rescue has about 820 civilian and uniformed members. Source for this fact about Prince William County
  • Manassas National Battlefield Park preserves the sites of the First Battle of Manassas (Bull Run) on July 21, 1861, and the Second Battle of Manassas on August 28 to 30, 1862. Source for this fact about Prince William County
