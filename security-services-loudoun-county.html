Security in Loudoun County

Loudoun County combines fast-growing suburbs, historic towns, farms, vineyards, and a major concentration of data centers. Washington Dulles International Airport sits along its border with Fairfax County, so many residents and employees travel often.

That mix creates a wide range of security needs, from restaurants and breweries with late-night crowds to schools and childcare facilities in new neighborhoods, houses of worship that welcome the public, and offices that handle sensitive information. I am a member of the Loudoun County Chamber of Commerce and work from Loudoun County, so I know these communities firsthand.

Loudoun County at a Glance

  • Leesburg is the county seat of Loudoun County, and the Loudoun County Government Center is at 1 Harrison Street SE in Leesburg. Source for this fact about Loudoun County
  • Loudoun County had 420,959 residents in the 2020 census, making it Virginia's third most populous county. Source for this fact about Loudoun County
  • Ashburn is an unincorporated community with a Silver Line Metro station, planned communities such as Ashburn Village and Ashburn Farm, and the NTSB's Ashburn Aviation Field Office. Source for this fact about Loudoun County
  • Loudoun is often called Data Center Alley, described as the world's largest concentration of data centers. Source for this fact about Loudoun County
  • Washington Dulles International Airport opened in 1962, and most of it lies within Loudoun County. Source for this fact about Loudoun County
