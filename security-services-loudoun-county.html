Security in Loudoun County

Loudoun County combines fast-growing suburbs, historic towns, farms, vineyards, a large concentration of data centers, and a major international airport, Washington Dulles International Airport. It also has a significant presence of federal government offices, federal government contractors, technology companies, and aerospace companies. Loudoun serves as both a bedroom community of Washington, DC, and an employment hub on its own.

That mix creates a wide range of security needs, including:

  • Restaurants, breweries, and wineries: late-night crowds, large events, and cash handling.
  • Schools and childcare facilities: emergency plans for threats, medical emergencies, and missing children.
  • Nonprofits and houses of worship: open-door gatherings, large events, and donations.
  • Offices, medical and law practices, and contractors: sensitive information, valuable equipment, and insider threats.
  • Retail stores and shopping centers: shoplifting, organized retail crime, and payment fraud.
  • Families and individuals: threats, harassment, home security, and fraud, forgery, impersonation, and other scams.

Crime Trends in Loudoun County

Crime in Loudoun County is generally low and fell overall in 2025, but fraud-related crimes and scams are rising. Fraud-type offenses, including forgery, impersonation, and wire fraud, are up 6% from 2024 to 2025. Forgery offenses are up 58%, impersonation offenses are up 26%, and wire fraud offenses are up 13%. Town police data shows the same trend. In Leesburg, forgery offenses are up 36% and impersonation offenses are up 25%. Middleburg and Purcellville, despite small numbers, saw increases in credit card fraud and forgery, and their combined violent crime decreased. Source for these crime statistics

The practical risk for most residents and businesses is financial fraud and identity crime, such as forged checks, credit card fraud, identity theft, impersonation scams, and account takeovers, not violent crime.

As an experienced criminal fraud investigator and Certified Fraud Examiner, I help organizations and families spot these schemes, tighten the controls that stop them, and investigate when one succeeds.
