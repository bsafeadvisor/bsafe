Security in Loudoun County

Loudoun County combines fast-growing suburbs, historic towns, farms, vineyards, and a major concentration of data centers. It also has a significant presence of federal government offices, federal government contractors, technology companies, and aerospace companies. Loudoun serves as both a bedroom community of Washington, DC, and an employment hub on its own. Washington Dulles International Airport lies largely within the county.

That mix creates a wide range of security needs:

  • Hospitality: Restaurants, breweries, and wineries with late-night crowds, events, and cash.
  • Schools and children's programs: Schools and childcare facilities in new neighborhoods that need plans for everything from a missing child to an active threat.
  • Nonprofits and houses of worship: Organizations that welcome the public and host large gatherings.
  • Offices and contractors: Medical and law practices, federal contractors, and technology firms that handle sensitive information and valuable equipment.
  • Retail: Shopping centers and stores that face theft and organized retail crime.
  • Families and individuals: Threats, harassment, home security, and fraud, forgery, impersonation, and other financial and identity scams, which are rising in the county.

Rapid growth adds new businesses, neighborhoods, traffic, and construction, so the risks change from year to year. As a Loudoun County resident and a member of the Loudoun County Chamber of Commerce, I know the diverse security needs of our community first-hand.

Crime Trends in Loudoun County

Crime is generally low and overall falling in Loudoun County, however fraud-type offenses are rising, including forgery, impersonation, and wire fraud. Here is what the most recent public numbers show for 2024 and 2025, the latest full years, and for 2026 so far.

  • Loudoun County reported 2,277 offenses per 100,000 residents in 2025, which is only 54% of Virginia's overall offense rate, and the fourth lowest of the 12 counties I serve. Source for this crime statistic
  • Violent crime fell 8.4%, and burglary fell 28%. Source for this crime statistic
  • However, fraud-type offenses, such as false pretenses, credit card fraud, identity theft, forgery, and impersonation, make up 18.7% of all offenses in Loudoun County, more than seven percentage points higher than Virginia as a whole. Source for this crime statistic
  • Between 2024 and 2025, forgery and counterfeiting offenses are up 58%, from 135 to 213, and impersonation offenses are up 27%, from 156 to 198, across the Sheriff's Office and the three town police departments. Source for this crime statistic

The practical risk for most residents and businesses is financial fraud and identity crime, such as forged checks, impersonation scams, and account takeovers, not violent crime. As a Certified Fraud Examiner, I help organizations and families spot these schemes, tighten the controls that stop them, and investigate when one succeeds. Learn about my services, including investigations, training, security audits, background investigations, travel security, and emergency planning.

Crime in the Town Police Jurisdictions

  • Leesburg: Shoplifting is the most common offense in Leesburg. About 28% of all reported theft offenses in Loudoun County occurred in Leesburg. White collar crime increased from 2024 to 2025, including a 36% increase in forgery offenses and a 25% increase in impersonation offenses. Leesburg retailers, restaurants, and any organization that accepts payments can reduce risk with my services, including security audits, training, investigations, and Security Director on Demand.
  • Middleburg and Purcellville: The relatively low number of offenses in each town makes percentage-based analysis potentially unreliable. Even so, white collar crimes such as fraud, credit card fraud, wire fraud, and forgery generally increased from 2024 to 2025, while violent crime decreased.

Counts are offenses reported to police, not arrests or people. Figures come from the Virginia State Police NIBRS crime reports and the Loudoun County Sheriff's Office crime dashboard. I update them on a regular basis as new data is available.

Loudoun County at a Glance

  • Leesburg is the county seat of Loudoun County. Source for this fact about Loudoun County
  • Within Loudoun County are the towns of Leesburg, Purcellville, Middleburg, Round Hill, Lovettsville, Hamilton, and Hillsboro.
  • Loudoun County had an estimated 447,837 residents in 2024, according to the county's Office of Management and Budget, making it Virginia's third most populous county. Source for the 2024 population estimate
  • Loudoun County is often called Data Center Alley. As of March 2026, Loudoun County had 56.5 million square feet of data center space built or under construction, with another 35.7 million approved. About 25% of North American data center capacity is in Northern Virginia. Source for Loudoun data center space Source for the Northern Virginia capacity share
  • Washington Dulles International Airport opened in 1962, and most of it lies within Loudoun County. Source for this fact about Loudoun County

How I Can Help

Security Director on Demand

Ongoing access to an experienced security leader, and all B Safe Advisor Group services, without the cost of a full-time hire.

Physical Security Audits

An independent review of your space, your procedures, and your high-risk areas, such as cash, high-value inventory, and sensitive information, followed by a written report that ranks each finding by risk, with recommendations. Audits can also include security penetration testing, assessment of current security arrangements, and audits of any security contracts or vendors.

Emergency Procedures

An audit of your current plans, new procedures where needed, and staff training and drills for critical incidents such as Active Threat Response, medical emergencies, fire, lockdown, evacuation, severe weather, and missing children.

Threat Assessment and Management

I assess and investigate threats and harassment, develop risk assessments, then help you manage and reduce the risk through a threat mitigation plan.

Private Investigations

Independent, confidential investigations of theft, fraud, inventory loss, threats, injuries and claims, allegations of staff or volunteer misconduct and policy violations, and the causes of accidents, fires, and damage to people or property. Houses of worship also use these services for lost donations and collection plate irregularities, and families use them for crimes against family members, stolen or missing property, and online harassment.

Training

A full range of training to keep you, your organization, and your family safe, including emergency medical response, Active Threat Response (commonly referred to as active shooter or armed intruder training), workplace violence prevention and recognition, fraud awareness and prevention, and retail theft and organized retail crime awareness. All B Safe Advisor Group training can be customized to meet your unique needs.

Travel Security

Destination-specific briefings and training for travelers and groups leaving from Dulles and beyond.

Background Investigations

Pre-employment and pre-volunteer screening matched to the position, from high-risk roles to executive roles and roles that handle money, plus due diligence, vendor screening, and executive, senior leader, and political candidate background investigations.

All services are available individually or as part of a Security Director on Demand contract.
