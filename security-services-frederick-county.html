Security in Frederick County

Frederick County surrounds the City of Winchester and combines fast-growing neighborhoods, distribution centers, orchards, and rural roads. Its location on Interstate 81 and Route 50 brings heavy traffic and many visitors, and growth has put more homes, schools, and businesses within reach of the same responders.

A clear emergency plan, a review of how people enter your building or property, and a trusted advisor you can call quickly help business owners, schools, and families decide what to do before something happens.

Frederick County at a Glance

  • Winchester, an independent city politically separate from the county, serves as the Frederick County seat. Source for this fact about Frederick County
  • Frederick County was created from Orange County in 1738, organized in 1743, and named for Frederick, Prince of Wales. Source for this fact about Frederick County
  • The 2020 census counted 91,419 residents across about 416 square miles. Source for this fact about Frederick County
  • Stephens City is the largest town in the county. Source for this fact about Frederick County
  • The county includes Civil War battlefield land from the Battle of Cedar Creek (October 1864) and the Third Battle of Winchester. Source for this fact about Frederick County
