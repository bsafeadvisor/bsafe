Emergency response planning

  • I audit, test, and review your current security measures and emergency procedures.
  • I develop and update contingency plans for threats, active shooters, large events, missing students, medical emergencies, and allegations of staff or student misconduct.
  • I conduct hands-on staff training and run emergency preparedness drills for staff and students.

Security Director on Demand

  • Direct access to an experienced Security Director for security and safety questions.
  • I act as your dedicated liaison with law enforcement, fire, and EMS agencies.
  • I conduct independent, confidential investigations of incidents, allegations, internal policy violations, and safety concerns.

Physical security assessment

  • An independent audit of your facilities, property, and current security measures.
  • I evaluate perimeter fencing, entry points, lighting, pick-up and drop-off areas, and visitor and parent check-in and access procedures.
  • I identify gaps, vulnerabilities, and risks, and I develop actionable, cost-effective solutions.

Background screening

  • Rigorous, multi-jurisdictional background screening of teachers, administrative staff, contractors, and recurring volunteers.
  • I use proprietary databases that are available only to licensed firms, and I analyze the results myself as a former law enforcement officer to find red flags that automatic and instant background check services miss.

About B Safe Advisor Group

B Safe Advisor Group provides physical security audits, threat assessment and management, emergency response planning and training, and independent investigations from Loudoun County, Virginia. I bring real-world experience as a former paramedic, firefighter, local law enforcement officer, and federal criminal investigator, and I do the work myself.
