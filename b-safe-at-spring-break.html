B Safe at Spring Break

A three-hour safety course that prepares students for a first spring break trip.

I am Zach Baumgart, a registered paramedic and former federal special agent with more than 25 years in public safety. I teach the course myself. About me

CallTextSend a Message

For Parents and Grandparents

Most students attend because a parent or grandparent signs them up. This course is for students who have never taken a spring break trip. It covers all aspects of spring break safety, including travel problems, water safety, nightlife safety, and how to get help, so your student leaves with a plan.

For Students

Short version: you will learn how to stay safe, look out for your friends, and know what to do if something goes wrong.

The Course

A three-hour course covering all aspects of safety and security concerns related to spring break travel for new travelers, including travel safety, management of delays and travel problems, water safety, nightlife safety including alcohol and drug safety, personal security, situational awareness, and how to get help.

  • Who: high school juniors and seniors and college freshmen and sophomores
  • Length: three hours, mostly lecture with some discussion

About Your Instructor

I have spent more than 25 years in public safety as a paramedic, firefighter, local law enforcement officer, and federal special agent. I also protected U.S. embassies and consulates overseas.
