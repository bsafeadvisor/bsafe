B Safe at Spring Break

A two-and-a-half-hour safety course that prepares students for a first spring break trip.

For Parents and Grandparents

Most students attend because a parent or grandparent signs them up. This course is for students who have never taken a spring break trip. It covers alcohol and drug safety and travel safety, so your student leaves with a plan.

For Students

Short version: you will learn how to stay safe, look out for your friends, and know what to do if something goes wrong.

The Course

  • Who: high school juniors and seniors and college freshmen and sophomores
  • Length: two and a half hours, mostly lecture with some discussion
  • Where: a local school building
  • Topics: alcohol and drug safety and travel safety

About Your Instructor
