The Course

A three-hour course covering the most common security and safety issues that lead to trouble and risky situations while on spring break. Among the topics covered are:

  • Alcohol and drug safety
  • Water safety
  • Nightlife risk awareness
  • Situational awareness
  • Handling emergencies
  • Travel management and border entry issues
  • How to get help
  • Overseas medical insurance (what to buy and how to use it)
  • Travel insurance (what to buy and how to use it)

The course is designed for students going on their first spring break trip, whether domestic or overseas, who are:

  • High school juniors and seniors
  • College freshmen and sophomores

Length: three hours, mostly lecture with built-in discussions and real-world examples of what can happen on spring break.

Cost: approximately $100 per student for prescheduled sessions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this course for?

Students going on their first spring break trip without family: high school juniors and seniors and college freshmen and sophomores.

How do I register?

Course dates for December 2026 and January 2027 are still being finalized. Please send me a message now to get on the notification list, and I will notify you directly when the course dates are set. Also keep an eye on my website for updates soon.

My student is traveling abroad. Is there more I can do?

Yes. I develop risk assessments and travel safety briefings for any international travel. Reach out to me today for more information or see my travel security page.

Can I book this course for a school, team, or other group?

Yes. In addition to the sessions that I will schedule, you can also schedule a private session for your own group. I can also adjust the course content to meet the specific needs of your group. Send me a message to learn more today. Also see my training page for additional details on custom training.

Where and how is the course taught?

All of my training is delivered in person. B Safe at Spring Break is held at set locations, which I will post here when scheduled. For private group classes, I can teach at your location or provide a space.

How much does the course cost?

Prescheduled sessions cost approximately $100 per student. Private sessions will be priced based on the number of attendees and the amount of custom content required.

Is there a course for students preparing to live away from home?

Yes. B Safe at College is a six-hour course for high school juniors, seniors, and graduates preparing to live away from home for the first time.
