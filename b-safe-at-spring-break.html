B Safe at Spring Break

A three-hour safety course that prepares students for a first spring break trip.

I am Zach Baumgart, a registered paramedic and former federal special agent with more than 25 years in public safety. I teach the course myself. About me

For Parents and Grandparents

Most students attend because a parent or grandparent signs them up. This course is for students who have never taken a spring break trip. Your student leaves with a plan for travel, water, nightlife, and getting help.

For Students

Short version: you will learn how to stay safe, look out for your friends, and know what to do if something goes wrong.

The Course

A three-hour course that prepares students for the safety challenges of their first spring break trip. Sessions are held in Northern Virginia. Topics include:

  • Travel safety and managing delays and travel problems
  • Water safety
  • Nightlife safety, including alcohol and drug safety
  • Personal security and situational awareness
  • How to get help
  • Who: first-time spring break travelers, including high school juniors and seniors and college freshmen and sophomores
  • Length: three hours, including lecture, discussion, and hands-on practical training

About Your Instructor

I have spent more than 25 years in public safety as a paramedic, firefighter, local law enforcement officer, and federal special agent. I also protected U.S. embassies and consulates overseas.

I was trained as an instructor by the Council of the Inspectors General on Integrity and Efficiency (CIGIE) and the U.S. Department of State's Diplomatic Security Training Center.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this course for?

Students who have never taken a spring break trip: high school juniors and seniors and college freshmen and sophomores. Most students attend because a parent or grandparent signs them up.

How do I register?

A parent or guardian registers and consents for any student under 18. I will post registration details when dates are scheduled.

My student is traveling abroad. Is there more I can do?

Yes. I brief individuals and families planning international travel. See my travel security page.

Can I book this course for a school, team, or other group?

Yes. I can teach B Safe at Spring Break to your group as a set course, and I can also build a custom course for your needs. See my training page, then send me a message or call or text me to start with a complimentary, confidential initial consultation.

Where is the course taught?

All of my training is delivered in person. B Safe at Spring Break is held at set locations, which I will post here when sessions are scheduled. For a group booking, I can teach at your location.

How much does the course cost?

Individual pricing will be posted with the session dates. For a group booking, there is no charge for the initial consultation and the proposal. I will discuss pricing during your initial consultation and provide you with the final pricing in the contract proposal.
