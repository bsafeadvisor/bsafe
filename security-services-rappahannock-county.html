Security in Rappahannock County

Rappahannock County is one of the most rural counties in Virginia, with mountain views, farms, estates, inns, and a small population spread across large properties. Neighbors can be far away, cell coverage can be uneven, and responders may need extra time to arrive.

Those conditions make planning matter even more. A clear emergency plan, a review of access to your property, and a trusted advisor you can call quickly help owners of estates, farms, inns, and homes decide what to do before something happens.

Rappahannock County at a Glance

  • Washington is the county seat and the county's only incorporated town; the 2020 Census counted 86 residents. Source for this fact about Rappahannock County
  • Rappahannock County had 7,348 residents at the 2020 Census and covers about 267 square miles. Source for this fact about Rappahannock County
  • The county was separated from Culpeper County by an Act of the General Assembly in 1833. Source for this fact about Rappahannock County
  • The Blue Ridge Mountains form the northwestern boundary and the Rappahannock River forms the northeastern boundary. Source for this fact about Rappahannock County
  • About 49.5 square miles of the county lie within Shenandoah National Park, and Skyline Drive runs along the Blue Ridge summits. Source for this fact about Rappahannock County
