What Is a Threat Assessment?

A threat assessment is a structured look at a threatening or concerning situation to decide how serious it is and what to do about it. Most threats are never carried out. However, only an assessment and investigation can determine the true intentions behind a threat and the likelihood that it will be carried out. I gather the facts, assess the level of risk, develop a comprehensive threat mitigation plan, and then work with you to decide what steps you want to take.

Threat assessments can also be conducted for workplace violence prevention, stalking, harassment, and other concerning behavior.

Who It Is For

Anyone facing a threat or concerning behavior, including businesses, nonprofits, schools, houses of worship, families, and individuals.

Common Situations

  • An employee, customer, or former partner has made a threat, or their behavior worries you.
  • You are ending a relationship with an employee, a client, or a business partner, and you are uncertain and concerned about the way the terminated party will respond.
  • Someone keeps contacting you after you asked them to stop, or you believe you are being stalked or followed.
  • Someone is harassing or intimidating you, your family, or your organization online.
  • A social media post or message comes from someone who worries you, or it contains content you perceive as a threat, intimidation, or coercion.
  • A dispute with a neighbor, vendor, or member of your community is escalating.
  • You are not sure whether what is happening is a threat, and you want a professional opinion.

What You Receive

A Written Report

My report will include an assessment of the risks, warning signs of escalation, and an assessment of whether the subject is capable of carrying out the threats.

A Threat Mitigation Plan

A plan that identifies definitive actions that can be taken to reduce the risk, as well as the signs of escalation that you should recognize. If appropriate, the plan will recommend what would trigger the need for an updated threat assessment and whether a regular review on a monthly, quarterly, or annual basis is recommended.

How It Works

1

First Consultation

We start with a confidential consultation by phone or virtual meeting lasting about 15 minutes. The consultation is complimentary for new clients.

2

Investigate and Assess

I investigate what happened by conducting interviews, collecting evidence, and developing facts. I use this information to develop a formal threat assessment and a threat mitigation plan.

3

Manage and Mitigate

We review the assessment and mitigation plan together, and I help you determine how to respond and implement the mitigation plan, with the goal of reducing the threat level over time.

4

Reevaluate

The threat mitigation plan will include an assessment of when reevaluation is recommended and tripwires that indicate an updated threat assessment is needed immediately.

Confidentiality

I handle every matter in confidence, and I will sign a nondisclosure agreement if you ask. If you are unsure whether your situation is a threat, call me, and I can talk it through with you.

Threat assessment does not replace 9-1-1 or law enforcement, and I do not provide legal advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if it is a threat?

If you feel that you, your family, your business, or your organization is being intimidated, coerced, or harassed, you may be experiencing a threat. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 9-1-1 immediately. Once the immediate danger is over, contact me to discuss whether a threat assessment is appropriate for your circumstances.

Will you contact the person making the threat?

Whether I contact the suspect depends on several factors, including the involvement of law enforcement, the nature of the threat, and your wishes. We discuss any contact with the suspect regularly during our meetings about the threat.

Will the police be involved?

If a law has been broken, local law enforcement may be involved. The level of involvement depends on the nature of the threat, the available evidence, and the availability of police resources. As a licensed private investigator in Virginia, I am authorized to conduct criminal and civil investigations, conduct surveillance, and gather evidence in support of potential criminal or civil judicial action. I am also authorized to conduct background investigations. At your request, I can conduct investigations independently of law enforcement action.

Can you work with my HR team or attorney?

Yes. In threat situations involving an employee or contractor, I almost always work with HR teams, ownership, attorneys, and others. When planning threat assessments and investigations, I often work directly with HR to determine what evidence is needed to take administrative action against the suspect.

Do you provide bodyguards or protective services?

No. I do not provide bodyguards or protective services. Personal protection in Virginia requires a separate qualification and license from the Department of Criminal Justice Services. If you request, I can help you find and work with a properly licensed personal protection firm.

How much does it cost?

Costs depend on the nature of the threat and the services requested. For example, a threat or harassment that occurs online generally costs less than an in-person threat by a known subject. I offer packages that include threat assessment, threat investigation, threat mitigation planning, and continual reevaluation of the situation and the suspect. I start with a flat upfront fee so we have a clear understanding of the threat and your needs. Then, by mutual consent, we enter into a fixed-cost contract for any additional services needed.

Prefer to Start with One Project?

Each of my services is available individually or as part of a Security Director on Demand contract. Threat assessment often leads to a private investigation, and training helps your staff recognize warning signs early. See my full list of services.
