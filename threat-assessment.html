Threat Assessment and Management

I assess and investigate threats and harassment, develop risk assessments, then help you manage and reduce the risk through a threat mitigation plan.

Who it is for

Organizations and individuals facing threats, harassment, or online harassment, including threats in the workplace.

How I work

  • Investigate. I look into what happened and who is involved.
  • Assess. I judge how serious the risk is and what could happen next.
  • Manage. I help you decide how to respond.
  • Mitigate. I build a plan that reduces the risk over time.

Confidentiality

I handle every matter in confidence. If you are unsure whether your situation is a threat, call me and we can talk it through.

Start with a confidential conversation.

The first consultation is complimentary for new clients, and every consultation is confidential. You will hear back directly within two business days.
