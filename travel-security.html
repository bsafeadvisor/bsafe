Travel Security

Based in Northern Virginia, I deliver destination-specific briefings that assess local risks for international travelers and set out practical measures to reduce them. I can provide briefings for any country, and I can also advise on choosing a destination.

Independent Advice, with Nothing to Sell

I do not sell security guards, alarms, cameras, or any other products or equipment. I only provide my services, so my recommendations are never tied to something I am selling. My only interest is giving you honest advice and the best client service.

Who I Serve

Individuals and Families

People planning international travel, especially first-time international travelers.

Businesses

Executives, employees, and teams traveling internationally for work.

Groups

Religious and nonprofit groups, including mission trips, as well as student groups, camps, and schools planning international travel.

What You Receive

My standard service is a written report that identifies areas of risk and mitigation strategies, and an in-person briefing to answer any questions you have. I also offer additional services including training for your travelers, daily monitoring of the safety and security situation of your destination starting two weeks before the trip and continuing until you return home, and availability for emergency consultations during your trip.

What the Briefing Covers

The briefing covers your concerns, the issues I see for your destination, and my recommendations. Topics include:

  • Crime and personal safety
  • Political unrest
  • Transportation
  • Lodging
  • Communications and digital security
  • Emergency contacts
  • Contingency plans
  • Medical emergencies

Training for Travelers

As an additional service, I provide training and briefings for the travelers, tailored to your destination and your group. The training builds on the risks and recommendations from your written report. The objective is for the travelers to minimize their risk and know what to do in an emergency.

Training topics include situational awareness, transportation safety, hotel safety, destination-specific risks, environmental risks, health and medical risks, and how to handle emergencies, along with other topics that are valuable for international travel and needed for your destination. See my training page for more details.

Destination Selection

If you are uncertain of your destination, let me help you identify a destination that balances risks versus your mission goals. I actively track security situations around the globe and maintain a robust network of contacts and sources of information to quickly identify risky destinations while offering other destinations that present less risk.

During the Trip

If you want support while you travel, I offer it as an additional service. I provide check-ins, daily monitoring of the regional and local security situation starting two weeks before the trip and continuing until you return home, and support in an emergency.

How It Works

1

Initial Consultation

A confidential consultation by phone or virtual meeting lasting about 15 minutes. The consultation is complimentary for new clients.

2

Scope and Contract

I recommend a scope that fits your trip, and a written contract that proposes the terms, costs, and services needed. Once we have a signed contract, I get to work to develop the risk assessment and briefing. A typical turnaround time is three to four weeks; however, I can complete the work faster for an additional fee.

3

Your Briefing

I prepare your destination-specific risk assessment and briefing, deliver the briefing in person, and give you a written report.

What I Need to Begin

To provide pricing and a contract proposal, I need some preliminary trip details:

  • The proposed destination
  • The proposed dates and length of the trip
  • The number of travelers
  • The services you need

After the contract is signed, to begin working I also need:

  • The purpose of the trip
  • The locations you plan to visit
  • The ages and a breakdown of the travelers
  • A draft itinerary, along with any other details you have

I prefer to begin at least six months before the trip. However, I can usually accommodate requests much closer to the departure date.

My Experience

As a federal special agent, I protected U.S. embassies and consulates around the world and developed security and safety plans for senior government officials traveling to destinations worldwide in some of the most challenging circumstances such as war, famine, transportation strikes, weather disasters, and more. These overseas experiences shape how I assess risk and develop recommendations to keep travelers safe.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this only for mission trips?

No. Mission trips are just one example of my travel security program. I also develop travel security programs and training for individuals, families, businesses, student groups, camps, and schools planning international travel.

What countries do you cover?

I can develop risk assessments and briefings for any country. I can also advise on selecting a destination that minimizes risks to the travelers.

How far ahead should I contact you?

I prefer at least six months before the trip. Typically, it takes three to four weeks to prepare a standard travel security risk assessment and briefing. I can complete it faster than the typical turnaround time for an additional fee.

Can you support us while we travel?

Yes, as an additional service. I provide check-ins, daily monitoring of the regional and local security situation starting two weeks before the trip and continuing until you return home, and support in an emergency. We discuss what support you want during your initial consultation.

How much does it cost?

After learning about your needs in our initial consultation, I can provide pricing and contract proposals. The cost depends on the destination, the services requested, and the number and type of trainings or briefings required. A written contract sets out the scope and terms before work begins.

Prefer to Start with One Project?

Each of my services is available individually or as part of a Security Director on Demand contract.
