No. Mission trips are one example. I also brief high-net-worth families and business leaders planning international travel.

Yes. If you want support while you travel, I can provide it. You and I discuss what support you want during your consultation.

The cost depends on your destination and the scope of the briefing and any support you want. A written contract sets out the scope and terms before I begin.
