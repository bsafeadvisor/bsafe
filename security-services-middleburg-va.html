Security and Investigation Needs in Middleburg

Middleburg combines a historic district, inns and restaurants, equestrian businesses, large private estates, and nearby wineries. That creates a wide range of needs, including:

  • Estates and private residences: remote property security, household staff screening, privacy, and emergency plans.
  • Inns, restaurants, and shops: theft, payment fraud, cash handling, and employee conduct.
  • Equestrian businesses, farms, and wineries: remote buildings, events, valuable equipment, and emergency plans.
  • Events and gatherings: access control and medical response.
  • Houses of worship, schools, and nonprofits: open-door gatherings, community events, volunteers, and donations.
  • Families and individuals: threats, harassment, home security, and scams that target residents.

The Middleburg Police Department reported 25 offenses in 2025, up from 18 in 2024. Wire fraud offenses rose from zero to seven and forgery offenses from one to four. Source: Virginia State Police NIBRS, calendar year 2025 compared with 2024 for these crime statistics
