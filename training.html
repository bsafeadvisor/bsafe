Custom Training Topics

Emergency Medical Response

As a registered paramedic, I teach your staff what to do in the first minutes of a medical emergency, before an ambulance arrives, so they can act with confidence and call for help the right way.

Active Threat Response

Active Threat Response, commonly referred to as active shooter or armed intruder training, prepares staff to respond. Details are in the section below.

Workplace Violence Prevention and Recognition

Staff learn the warning signs of escalating behavior, how and when to report a concern, and how a workplace response plan supports them.

Pre-Assault Indicators

Staff learn to recognize the behaviors that often come before an assault, so they can act early, stay safe, and call for help.

Fraud Awareness and Prevention

As a Certified Fraud Examiner (CFE), I explain how common schemes work, such as payment fraud, skimming, and business email compromise. Staff learn the red flags and the steps to take when something looks wrong.

Indicators of Embezzlement and Other High-Risk Behavior

Managers and staff learn the warning signs of embezzlement and other high-risk behavior, and how and when to report a concern.

Retail Theft and Organized Retail Crime (ORC) Awareness

As a member of the Mid-Atlantic Organized Retail Crime Alliance (MAORCA), I teach retail staff to recognize theft behavior and organized crime patterns and to respond safely, including when not to intervene.

Online Harassment and Personal Safety for Families

I help parents and families understand online harassment, document it, limit their exposure, and decide when to involve the platform's accountability and safety team or law enforcement.

De-escalation

Staff learn to spot a risky situation, use calm communication to lower tension, and set clear boundaries. They also learn when to step away and call for help.

Crowd Management

Staff and volunteers learn how to monitor a crowd, spot early signs of trouble, and manage entrances, exits, and busy events safely.

Travel Safety for International Travelers

Briefings and security training, customized for your destination, for travelers headed abroad. Learn about my travel security service.

Active Threat Response Training

Active Threat Response prepares people to make good decisions in the first moments of an attack, when seconds matter the most. I teach your staff to recognize warning signs and to understand their response options, including how to get out, how to secure a space, and what to do as a last resort. The session also covers how to communicate with co-workers and with 9-1-1, what to expect when law enforcement arrives, and basic emergency medical care until help reaches you. Drills and hands-on practice can be built into any training session to reinforce what staff learn.

I also offer reviews and updates to your organization's Active Threat Response plans as part of my emergency procedures and Security Director on Demand services.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who should attend a training session?

Anyone in your organization, including front-line staff, managers, volunteers, and board members. I tailor each session to the group.

Where does the training take place?

All of my training is delivered in person, on-site at your workplace or another location of your choice.

How long are sessions, and how large are the classes?

B Safe at Spring Break runs three hours, and B Safe at College runs six hours. For organizations, your needs set the session length, course type, and class size.

How much does it cost?

The cost depends on the course type, length, and class size. There is no charge for the initial consultation and the proposal. I will discuss pricing during your initial consultation and provide you with the final pricing in the contract proposal.

Can you teach a topic that is not on your list?

Yes. The topics above are examples. Tell me what your group needs during your initial consultation, and I will build a session to fit.

Does the emergency medical response training lead to a certification?

No. It is not a certification course, but it does build life-saving skills.

Can you schedule training around our operations?

I work with you during your initial consultation to find a time that fits your operations.

Do I need an audit or emergency plan before I book training?

No. Training is available on its own or as part of a larger contract. I also offer services to draft, develop, and review emergency plans.

Do you offer courses for students?

Yes. B Safe at Spring Break and B Safe at College are courses for teens and young adults. See each course page for details.

Do you offer refresher training?

Yes. I offer annual or as-needed sessions, which keeps new staff trained and earlier lessons fresh.

Will Active Threat Response training frighten my staff?

I teach in a calm, practical way and adjust the content for the group, including schools, childcare facilities, and camps.

Do you receive anything from the products or vendors you recommend?

No. I sell only my services and receive no referral fees or commissions. The only one who pays me is you.

How soon can we get started?

I respond within two business days of your request, and I begin with a complimentary, confidential initial consultation.
