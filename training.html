Independent Advice, with Nothing to Sell

I do not sell security guards, alarms, cameras, or any other products or equipment. I only provide my services, so my training is never tied to something I am selling. My only interest is giving you honest advice and the best client service.

Who I Serve

Organizations

Businesses, nonprofits, schools, childcare facilities, camps, and houses of worship that want their people prepared.

Individuals and Families

Online harassment and personal safety for families, and travel safety for international travelers.

Students

Courses for teens and young adults, from spring break trips to living on their own.

Two Ways to Train

With My Other Services

Training is built into my physical security audits, emergency procedures, and threat assessment work, and proposals come with my audit reports and emergency plans.

Stand-Alone Training

Your needs set the course type, length, and class size. I offer annual or as-needed sessions.

Custom Training Topics

  • Emergency medical response
  • Active shooter and active threat response
  • Workplace violence prevention and recognition
  • Fraud awareness and prevention
  • Retail theft and organized retail crime awareness
  • Online harassment and personal safety for families
  • Travel safety for international travelers

Active Threat Response Training

Active shooter and armed intruder preparedness for staff, covering recognition, response options, communication, and drills. See emergency procedures for the plans that go with it.

Courses for Students

B Safe at Spring Break

A two-and-a-half-hour course on alcohol, drug, and travel safety for students heading on a first spring break trip.

B Safe at College

A five- to seven-hour course for high school juniors and seniors who are about to live on their own.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does the training take place?

I deliver training on-site at your workplace. My student courses are held in local school buildings.

Can you teach a topic that is not on your list?

Yes. The topics above are examples. Tell me what your group needs during your initial consultation, and I will build a session to fit.

How long are sessions, and how large are the classes?

Your needs set the course type, length, and class size.

Do you offer courses for students?

Yes. B Safe at Spring Break and B Safe at College are courses for teens and young adults. See each course page for details.

How much does it cost?

The cost depends on the course type, length, and class size. I give you the price during your initial consultation, before you decide.

Prefer to Start with One Project?

Each of my services is available individually or as part of a Security Director on Demand contract. Training can often lead to or also require emergency procedures, threat assessment, and physical security audits. See my full list of services.

Start with a Confidential Conversation.

The initial consultation is complimentary for new clients, and every consultation is confidential. I do not sell guards, alarms, or equipment, so you get independent advice. You will hear back directly within two business days.
