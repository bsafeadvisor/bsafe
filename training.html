Custom Training Topics

Emergency Medical Response

As a registered paramedic, I teach your staff what to do in the first minutes of a medical emergency, before an ambulance arrives, so they can act with confidence and call for help the right way.

Active Threat Response

Active shooter and armed intruder preparedness for staff. Details are in the section below.

Workplace Violence Prevention and Recognition

Staff learn the warning signs of escalating behavior, how and when to report a concern, and how a workplace response plan supports them.

Fraud Awareness and Prevention

As a Certified Fraud Examiner (CFE), I explain how common schemes work, such as payment fraud, skimming, and business email compromise. Staff learn the red flags and the steps to take when something looks wrong.

Retail Theft and Organized Retail Crime (ORC) Awareness

As a member of the Mid-Atlantic Organized Retail Crime Alliance (MAORCA), I teach retail staff to recognize theft behavior and organized crime patterns and to respond safely, including when not to intervene.

Online Harassment and Personal Safety for Families

I help parents and families understand online harassment, document it, limit their exposure, and decide when to involve the platform's accountability and safety team or law enforcement.

De-escalation

Staff learn to spot a risky situation, use calm communication to lower tension, and set clear boundaries. They also learn when to step away and call for help.

Travel Safety for International Travelers

Briefings and security training for travelers headed abroad that are customized for your destination(s). Learn about my travel security service.

Active Threat Response Training

Active threat response training, commonly referred to as active shooter or armed intruder training, prepares people to make good decisions in the first moments of an attack, when seconds matter the most. I teach your staff to recognize warning signs and to understand their response options, including how to get out, how to secure a space, and what to do as a last resort. The session also covers how to communicate with co-workers and with 9-1-1, what to expect when law enforcement arrives, and basic emergency medical care until help reaches you. Drills are available when your group is ready for them.

See emergency procedures for the plans that go with this training.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where does the training take place?

I deliver training on-site at your workplace. My student courses are held in local school buildings.

Can you teach a topic that is not on your list?

Yes. The topics above are examples. Tell me what your group needs during your initial consultation, and I will build a session to fit.

How long are sessions, and how large are the classes?

Your needs set the course type, length, and class size.

Do you offer courses for students?

Yes. B Safe at Spring Break and B Safe at College are courses for teens and young adults. See each course page for details.

How much does it cost?

The cost depends on the course type, length, and class size. I give you the price during your initial consultation, before you decide.

Who should attend a training session?

Anyone in your organization, including front-line staff, managers, volunteers, and board members. I tailor each session to the group.

Can you schedule training around our operations?

I work with you during your initial consultation to find a time that fits your operations.

Do I need an audit or emergency plan before I book training?

No. Training is available on its own or as part of a larger contract.

Do you offer refresher training?

Yes. I offer annual or as-needed sessions, which keeps new staff trained and earlier lessons fresh.

Will the active threat training frighten my staff?

I teach in a calm, practical way and adjust the content for the group, including schools, childcare facilities, and camps.

Do you receive anything from the products or vendors you recommend?

No. I sell only my services and receive no referral fees or commissions.

How soon can we get started?

I respond within two business days of your request, and we begin with a complimentary, confidential initial consultation.

Prefer to Start with One Project?

Each of my services is available individually or as part of a Security Director on Demand contract. Training can often lead to or also require emergency procedures, threat assessment, and physical security audits. See my full list of services.

Start with a Confidential Conversation.

The initial consultation is complimentary for new clients, and every consultation is confidential. I do not sell guards, alarms, or equipment, so you get independent advice. You will hear back directly within two business days.
