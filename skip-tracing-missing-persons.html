Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between skip tracing and a missing person search?

Skip tracing locates someone who cannot be reached, often to recover money or property. A missing person search helps a family or an attorney find someone who is missing. Both rely on careful, lawful methods.

Can you promise to find the person?

No. I cannot promise a result, but I use licensed investigative methods, and I confirm that I have found the right person before I report to you.

How much does it cost?

The cost depends on the scope of the search. A written contract sets out the scope and terms before I begin.

Prefer to Start with One Project?
