Security in Warren County

Warren County sits where the Blue Ridge meets the Shenandoah Valley, with Front Royal as its hub and Skyline Drive and the Shenandoah River drawing visitors year-round. Interstate 66 and Interstate 81 bring heavy travel through the county, and much of the land is rural or wooded.

Those conditions make planning matter. A review of access to your property, a clear emergency plan, and a trusted advisor you can call quickly help owners of businesses, lodging, rental properties, and homes decide what to do before something happens.

Warren County at a Glance

  • Front Royal is the county seat of Warren County and the only incorporated town in the county; it was incorporated in 1788. Source for this fact about Warren County
  • Warren County was established in 1836 from parts of Frederick and Shenandoah Counties. Source for this fact about Warren County
  • The 2020 census counted 40,727 residents in Warren County, which covers about 217 square miles. Source for this fact about Warren County
  • Warren County borders six other jurisdictions: Frederick, Clarke, Fauquier, Rappahannock, Page, and Shenandoah Counties. Source for this fact about Warren County
  • Front Royal sits at the confluence of the North Fork and South Fork of the Shenandoah River. Source for this fact about Warren County
