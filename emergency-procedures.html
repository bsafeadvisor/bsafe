Frequently Asked Questions

What if we already have plans?

That is a good start. I review your current plans, identify any gaps, and recommend changes. If your plans are sound, I will tell you so. I will also recommend drills and training for your staff and, if requested, facilitate and lead them.

How is this different from training?

Emergency procedures define what your people do in each situation, and drills prove they can do it. Training builds specific skills, such as emergency medical response, de-escalation, and Active Threat Response. The two work well together.

What kinds of drills do you run?

Drills can be tabletop or live, and I can run them for any emergency plan, including Active Threat Response, medical emergencies, emergency evacuation, and others. Every drill includes a written after-action report that identifies any recommended changes to your current emergency plans.

How often should plans be reviewed?

I recommend reviewing plans annually and after any incident. Annual reviews and updates are a separate contract, or are included with a Security Director on Demand contract.

Do you work with local fire, EMS, and police agencies?

Yes. I will work with your local first responders to ensure your emergency procedures align with their expectations.

How much does it cost?

The cost depends on the size of your organization, the number of locations, and the situations you want covered. I give you the price during your consultation, before you decide.

Prefer to Start with One Project?

Each of my services is available individually or as part of a Security Director on Demand contract. Emergency procedures can often lead to or also require physical security audits, threat assessment, and training. See my full list of services.

Start with a Confidential Conversation.

The initial consultation is complimentary for new clients, and every consultation is confidential. I do not sell guards, alarms, or equipment, so you get independent advice. You will hear back directly within two business days.
