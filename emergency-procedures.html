Emergency Procedures

An audit of your current plans, new procedures where you need them, and training and drills so your staff know what to do.

For child-serving organizations

If you care for children, you also plan for the situations parents worry about most, including a missing child or camper. I build procedures for private schools, childcare facilities, camps, and children's activity venues with those risks in mind.

Who it is for

Businesses, private schools, childcare facilities, camps, children's venues, nonprofits, and houses of worship.

Three parts

  • Audit. I review the procedures you have now.
  • Develop. I write new procedures where gaps exist.
  • Train and drill. I train your staff and run drills so the procedures work under pressure.

Situations covered

  • Active shooters
  • Medical emergencies
  • Fire
  • Lockdown
  • Evacuation
  • Severe weather
  • Missing children or campers
