Independent Advice, with Nothing to Sell

I do not sell security guards, alarms, cameras, or any other products or equipment. I only provide my services, so my advice is never tied to something I am selling. My only interest is giving you honest advice and the best client service.

How I Help Camps

Emergency Response Planning

  • I audit, test, and review your current security measures and emergency procedures.
  • I develop and update contingency plans for threats, Active Threat Response (commonly referred to as active shooter or armed intruder training), severe weather, missing campers, medical emergencies, claims of staff or camper misconduct, and parent reunification.
  • I conduct hands-on staff training and run emergency preparedness drills.

Security Director on Demand

  • Direct access to an experienced Security Director for security and safety questions.
  • I act as your dedicated liaison with law enforcement, fire, and EMS agencies.
  • I conduct independent, confidential investigations of incidents, allegations, internal policy violations, and safety concerns.

Physical Security Assessment

  • An independent audit of your facilities, property, cabins, and current security measures.
  • I evaluate perimeter fencing, entry points, lighting, pick-up and drop-off areas, and visitor check-in procedures.
  • I identify gaps, vulnerabilities, and risks, and I develop actionable, cost-effective solutions.

Background Screening

  • Rigorous, multi-jurisdictional background screening of counselors, administrative staff, contractors, and recurring volunteers.
  • I use proprietary databases that are available only to licensed firms, and I analyze the results myself as a former law enforcement officer to find red flags that automatic and instant background check services miss.
