Security Needs in Herndon

Herndon combines a historic downtown, the W&OD Trail, office parks, and neighborhoods close to Dulles and the Route 28 corridor. That creates a wide range of needs, including:

  • Downtown shops, restaurants, and services: theft, payment fraud, cash handling, and employee conduct.
  • Technology, aerospace, and contractor offices: sensitive information, valuable equipment, and insider threats.
  • Schools, childcare facilities, and camps: emergency plans for threats, medical emergencies, and missing children.
  • Houses of worship and community groups: large gatherings, festivals, volunteers, and donations.
  • Employees and families who travel: destination briefings and training for trips from nearby Dulles.
  • Families and individuals: threats, harassment, home security, and scams that target residents.

Crime and Fraud Risks in Herndon

The Town of Herndon has its own police department, so its crime is reported separately from Fairfax County Police Department figures. Fraud, theft, and scams affect every community in Northern Virginia, and one incident can cause a large loss for a small business or a family. For county-wide figures, see my Fairfax County crime trends.

Fraud crimes reported to the Herndon Police Department fell about 17% in 2025, to 87 from 105, and larceny reports fell about 29%, to 290 from 408. Theft from motor vehicles fell 43%, from 117 to 67, but shoplifting stayed high, at 107 reports compared with 112 in 2024. False pretenses and impersonation scams were most of the fraud, at 38 and 33 reports. Burglary fell from 25 reports to 17, but motor vehicle theft rose from 25 to 33.

Violent crime reports rose from 64 to 69. Aggravated assault rose from 43 to 50, while robbery fell from 15 to 13. Herndon recorded one murder in 2025 and none in 2024.

Fraud and theft can reach any business or family in Herndon.
