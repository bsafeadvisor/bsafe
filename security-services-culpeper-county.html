Security in Culpeper County

Culpeper County combines a busy county seat with growing neighborhoods, farms, and historic sites. Route 29 and Route 15 carry heavy traffic through the county, and growth in housing and commerce has increased the number of schools, shops, and businesses that need a plan.

A clear emergency plan, a review of how people enter your building or property, and a trusted advisor you can call quickly help business owners, schools, houses of worship, and families decide what to do before something happens.

Culpeper County at a Glance

  • Culpeper County was founded in 1749, and the Town of Culpeper is its county seat. Source for this fact about Culpeper County
  • The county covers about 383 square miles and had 52,552 residents in the 2020 census. Source for this fact about Culpeper County
  • The Town of Culpeper was surveyed in 1749 by a young George Washington and had 20,062 residents in the 2020 census. Source for this fact about Culpeper County
  • The town sits at the intersection of U.S. Routes 15 and 29, and U.S. Route 522 also serves the area. Source for this fact about Culpeper County
  • Amtrak's Culpeper station is served by the Cardinal, Northeast Regional, and Crescent trains. Source for this fact about Culpeper County
