Security in Shenandoah County

Shenandoah County runs along the Interstate 81 corridor, with small towns, farms, poultry operations, vineyards, and mountain resorts. Visitors come for the caverns, the Shenandoah River, and the ski and lake communities near Basye, and some properties sit empty for much of the year.

Those conditions make planning matter. A review of access to your property, a clear emergency plan, and a trusted advisor you can call quickly help owners of businesses, resorts, rental homes, and farms decide what to do before something happens.

Shenandoah County at a Glance

  • Woodstock is the county seat of Shenandoah County, with Revolutionary War and Civil War history. Source for this fact about Shenandoah County
  • Shenandoah County covers about 512 square miles and has a population of nearly 45,000 residents. Source for this fact about Shenandoah County
  • The Shenandoah County Historic Courthouse is listed as a county landmark. Source for this fact about Shenandoah County
  • New Market, the southernmost town in the county, was the site of the 1864 Battle of New Market. Source for this fact about Shenandoah County
  • Edinburg is a preserved small town with an 1848 mill. Source for this fact about Shenandoah County
