B Safe at Spring Break: Attendee Resources

This page is for students and families registered for the course. Please do not share the link or passcode.

Course Details

  • Dates: Monday, December 21, and Tuesday, December 22, 2026, 9 AM to 12 PM (you registered for one session)
  • Location: Brickyard Ashburn, 44927 George Washington Blvd, Suite 265, Ashburn, VA 20147
  • Consent form: Every student under 18 on the day of the course needs a consent form signed by a parent or guardian. Students who are 18 or older sign their own. I email the form after you register.

Checklists

Complete these before your trip. Download a copy, fill it in, and keep it with you.

Traveler’s Predeparture Checklist

For students. It covers your passport, phone setup, insurance, your group and buddy, your personal limits, money, rides, and your exit plan.

Download the Traveler’s Checklist (Word)

Parent’s Predeparture Checklist

For parents and guardians. It covers contact information, daily check-ins, insurance, group and hotel details, and Smart Traveler Enrollment Program (STEP) enrollment.

Download the Parent’s Checklist (Word)

Ground Rules for the Course

  • No shame and no judgment. Every question is a good question.
  • What is shared stays here. Please keep what others say confidential.
  • No recording or photos. Please keep phones away during discussion.
  • Step out anytime. You do not need to explain, especially during sensitive modules.
