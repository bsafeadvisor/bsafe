Ashburn

Being based locally, I work with Ashburn clients regularly. Whether you are in Ashburn Farm, Belmont, Brambleton, Broadlands, One Loudoun, or one of the office parks and shopping centers along Route 7 and the Dulles Greenway, I can meet you at your location.

Security and Investigation Needs in Ashburn

Ashburn is one of the fastest-growing communities in Virginia. It is known locally as Data Center Alley, and a 2023 market report calls it North America's major data center market, with about 25 million square feet of commercial space (Source). Ashburn also has a Metrorail Silver Line station, plus neighborhoods and shopping centers that serve many technology and federal contracting professionals. Washington Dulles International Airport is about seven miles away. That creates a wide range of needs, including:

  • Executives, professionals, and their families: personal and family security, fraud and identity crime, threats and harassment, and travel security from Dulles.
  • Small businesses: theft, payment fraud, employee misconduct, and emergency plans for stores, restaurants, medical and dental offices, and professional offices.
  • Data centers and their vendors: insider threats, contractor and visitor access, and theft of valuable equipment.
  • Companies serving the technology industry: background investigations, vendor screening, and investigations of fraud and insider threats.
  • Schools, childcare facilities, and camps: emergency plans for threats, medical emergencies, and missing children.
  • Houses of worship and nonprofits: large gatherings, open-door events, volunteers, and donations.
  • Households and neighborhoods: prevention and investigation of financial fraud, threats and harassment (online or in person), and other crimes. In addition, the risks posed by home security vulnerabilities, package and vehicle theft, and property crimes.

I can audit physical security, help screen vendors and contractors, and investigate losses.

Crime Trends in Ashburn

Ashburn is unincorporated and served by the Loudoun County Sheriff's Office Ashburn Station. In 2025, larceny was the largest property category at the station, with 485 crimes, along with 234 destruction of property crimes, 65 auto thefts, and 16 burglaries. Source: Loudoun County Sheriff's Office crime dashboard, calendar year 2025 for these station statistics

Working with Local Agencies in Ashburn

Sterling

Sterling is an unincorporated community in eastern Loudoun County, adjacent to Washington Dulles International Airport, with business and industrial parks, retail centers, and large residential neighborhoods, stretching to the Potomac River.

Security and Investigation Needs in Sterling

Sterling has more commercial space than any other area in a 2023 Loudoun County market report, about 33 million of the county's 87 million square feet (Source). It combines airport-related businesses, warehouses and flex space, shopping centers, and family neighborhoods along the Route 7 and Route 28 corridors. That creates a wide range of needs, including:

  • Warehouses, logistics, and flex-space businesses: theft, inventory loss, after-hours access, insider threats, and employee misconduct.
  • Airport-related and contractor businesses: background investigations, vendor screening, insider threats, and protecting sensitive information.
  • Retail stores and restaurants: shoplifting, employee theft, cash handling, and payment fraud.
  • Schools, childcare facilities, and camps: emergency plans for threats, medical emergencies, and missing children.
  • Houses of worship and nonprofits: large gatherings, community events, volunteers, and donations.
  • Employees and families: threats, harassment, financial crimes, stolen property, and scams.

Warehouses and shipping operations face inventory loss and insider threats, while families and small businesses face the same scams that reach everyone. I can audit your site, investigate losses, and train your staff.

Crime Trends in Sterling

Sterling is served by the Loudoun County Sheriff's Office Eastern Loudoun Station. In 2025, in the Eastern Loudoun Station, crimes against persons and property made up 41% of all instances reported to the Loudoun County Sheriff's Office. Theft represents about half of those crimes, with 863 larceny crimes, 99 auto thefts, and 35 burglaries, along with 321 destruction of property crimes. Source: Loudoun County Sheriff's Office crime dashboard, calendar year 2025 for these station statistics

Working with Local Agencies in Sterling

Purcellville

Purcellville is a town in western Loudoun County, about nine miles west of Leesburg, in the Loudoun Valley with views of the Blue Ridge. Whether you are in downtown Purcellville, at a farm or winery nearby, or in another western Loudoun community, I can meet you at your location.

Security and Investigation Needs in Purcellville and Western Loudoun

A 2023 market report describes Purcellville as the economic hub of western Loudoun County, with more than 800 active businesses (Source). It combines a historic downtown, the western end of the W&OD Trail, Patrick Henry College, public schools, farms, and nearby wineries and breweries. In a close-knit community, people trust each other, which can make fraud and internal theft harder to see. Properties can also be spread out, so emergency plans must account for a longer response time from emergency services. Needs include:

  • Downtown shops, restaurants, and offices: theft, payment fraud, cash handling, and employee misconduct.
  • Wineries, breweries, farms, and event venues: crowds, alcohol-related incidents, cash handling, remote property security, and emergency plans.
  • Schools, childcare facilities, and camps: emergency plans for threats, medical emergencies, and missing children.
  • Houses of worship and nonprofits: open-door gatherings, community events, volunteers, and donations.
  • Medical, dental, and professional offices: sensitive information, workplace threats, and insider threats.
  • Families and rural properties: isolated property, home security, harassment, and fraud and other scams.

Community events, visitors, and seasonal crowds bring a steady mix of customers, employees, and volunteers to local businesses and organizations. I can review your site, plan for emergencies, and investigate theft, fraud, or misconduct when it occurs.

Crime Trends in Purcellville

The Purcellville Police Department reported 29 larceny crimes in 2025, down from 58, and 31 fraud crimes, down from 33. Source: Virginia State Police NIBRS, calendar year 2025 compared with 2024 for these crime statistics

Middleburg

Middleburg is a small historic town in western Loudoun County, along U.S. Route 50, known as the "Nation's Horse and Hunt Capital." Whether you are in town, on an estate, or at a farm or winery nearby, I can meet you at your location.

Security and Investigation Needs in Middleburg

Middleburg combines a historic district, inns and restaurants, equestrian businesses, large private estates, and nearby wineries. That creates a wide range of needs, including:

  • Estates and private residences: remote property security, household staff screening, privacy, and emergency plans.
  • Inns, restaurants, and shops: theft, payment fraud, cash handling, and employee misconduct.
  • Equestrian businesses, farms, and wineries: remote buildings, events, valuable equipment, and emergency plans.
  • Events and gatherings: access control and medical response.
  • Houses of worship, schools, and nonprofits: open-door gatherings, community events, volunteers, and donations.
  • Families and individuals: threats, harassment, home security, and scams that target residents.

Visitors, seasonal events, and properties with valuable art, horses, and equipment create privacy and security needs that differ from a typical suburb. I can assess your property, plan for emergencies, and investigate losses or threats confidentially.

Crime Trends in Middleburg

Middleburg is a small town served by the Middleburg Police Department, which reported ten fraud crimes in 2025, up from four in 2024, and four larceny crimes, up from three.

Working with Local Agencies in Middleburg

Loudoun County Crime Trends

Violent crime across Loudoun County fell 8% from 2024 to 2025, from 441 to 404 reports. Source: Virginia State Police NIBRS for violent crime counts

Across Loudoun County, fraud-related crimes are up 6% from 2024 to 2025.

  • Forgery crimes are up 58%, from 135 to 213.
  • Impersonation crimes are up 26%, from 157 to 198.
  • Wire fraud crimes are up 13%, from 112 to 127.

Source: Virginia State Police NIBRS, calendar year 2025 compared with 2024 for these crime statistics

The practical risks for Loudoun County businesses and families are fraud, theft, and vandalism. Even a few crimes can mean large losses, because a single forged check, payment scam, or employee theft can hurt a small business or a family badly.

As a Certified Fraud Examiner and former federal criminal investigator, I help organizations and families spot these schemes, tighten the controls that stop them, and investigate when a loss occurs. Learn about my investigations, security audits, training, and Security Director on Demand.
