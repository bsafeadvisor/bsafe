Security in Page County

Page County lies between the Blue Ridge and Massanutten Mountain, with Luray as its county seat. Skyline Drive, Luray Caverns, and the Shenandoah River bring many visitors, and the county's lodging, rentals, and small businesses see heavy seasonal traffic.

Those conditions make planning matter. A review of access to your property, a clear emergency plan, and a trusted advisor you can call quickly help owners of lodging, attractions, businesses, and homes decide what to do before something happens.

Page County at a Glance

  • Luray is the county seat of Page County, which was formed in 1831 from parts of Shenandoah and Rockingham counties. Source for this fact about Page County
  • The county is named for John Page, Governor of Virginia from 1802 to 1805. Source for this fact about Page County
  • The 2020 census counted 23,709 residents in Page County. Source for this fact about Page County
  • Page County covers about 314 square miles, with Hawksbill Mountain, inside Shenandoah National Park, as its highest point. Source for this fact about Page County
  • Part of the county lies within the George Washington National Forest. Source for this fact about Page County
