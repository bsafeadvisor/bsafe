Independent advice, with nothing to sell

I do not sell security guards, alarms, cameras, or any other products or equipment. I provide only my services, so my findings are never tied to something I am selling. My only interest is giving you an honest assessment and recommendations that fit your needs and budget.

Who it is for

Businesses and organizations

Any business or organization with a physical space, cash, valuable inventory, or sensitive information.

Families and individuals

Homes, estates, and second residences. I review entrances, lighting, locks, alarms, cameras, and routines, and I recommend practical improvements that fit how your family lives.

What the audit covers

Your space

Entrances, lighting, locks, sight lines, access control, and how people move through the building.

Your procedures

Whether your staff follow the procedures you already have.

High-risk areas

Cash, high-value inventory, and sensitive information.

What you receive

A written report

The report includes photographs and ranks each finding by assessed level of risk, so you know what to fix first. It also includes my recommendations for improvement and proposals on how to address any concerns.

Testing, if you want it

Audits can also include testing of your physical security, such as attempts to enter without authorization, and an assessment of your current security arrangements.

Already have a guard company or alarm vendor?

I can audit whether you are getting what you pay for. I review your security contracts and vendors against what they promise and what they deliver, and I report the results to you.

Experience behind the audit

At U.S. embassies and consulates overseas, I was responsible for physical security and emergency planning, including access control, management of security services contracts, management and deployment of armed guard forces, control of CCTV and alarm systems, key control procedures, and the secure storage of sensitive and classified information. I bring the same discipline to your building, your contractors, and your procedures.

Frequently asked questions

How long does an audit take?

It depends on the size and complexity of your site. I give you an estimate during your consultation, before you decide.

How much does it cost?

The fee depends on the scope. I give you the price during your consultation, before you decide.

Will my staff know?

That is your decision. Some parts of an audit work best when staff are not told in advance, so I agree on the approach with ownership or management beforehand.

Do you recommend specific products?

I recommend what you need, not specific brands. I sell no equipment and earn nothing from any product, so my advice stays independent.

Can you audit more than one location?

Yes. We decide the scope together during your consultation.

Is an audit a one-time project?

It can be. You can order an audit as a one-off project or include it in an ongoing Security Director on Demand contract.

Prefer to start with one project?

Absolutely. Each of my services is available individually or as part of a Security Director on Demand contract. Many clients pair an audit with emergency procedures, threat assessment, and staff training. See my full list of services.

Start with a confidential conversation.

The first consultation is complimentary for new clients, and every consultation is confidential. I do not sell guards, alarms, or equipment, so you get independent advice. You will hear back directly within two business days.
