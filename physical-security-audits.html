Independent Advice, with Nothing to Sell

I do not sell security guards, alarms, cameras, or any other products or equipment. I only provide my services, so my findings are never tied to something I am selling. My only interest is giving you an honest assessment and recommendations that fit your needs and budget.

Who It Is For

Businesses and Organizations

Any business or organization with a physical space, cash, valuable inventory, or sensitive information.

Families and Individuals

Homes, estates, and second residences. I review entrances, lighting, locks, alarms, cameras, and routines, and I recommend practical improvements that fit how your family lives.

What the Security Audit Covers

Your Space

Entrances, lighting, sight lines, access control, and how people move through the building; current security arrangements and procedures, including CCTV, locks, alarms, and the use of security contractors; and surrounding areas, including parking lots and adjacent spaces.

Your Procedures

An assessment of your current procedures, whether your staff and security contractors follow them, and whether they are effective.

High-Risk Areas

An assessment of how cash and sensitive information are handled; how high-value or easily stolen inventory is stored and accessed; receiving areas; and sensitive areas, including financial management and human resources spaces.

What You Receive

A Written Report

The report includes photographs and ranks each finding by assessed level of risk, so you know your biggest risks and vulnerabilities. It also includes my recommendations for improvement and proposals on how to address any concerns.

Testing

Security audits can also include testing of your physical security systems and processes, such as attempts to gain access to limited-access areas or determining whether staff and security contractors are following established procedures.

Already Have a Guard Company or Alarm Vendor?

I can audit your security contracts and vendors to ensure they are providing services in accordance with your contracts. I report the results to you, and if you choose, I can engage with the security contractor or vendor directly to address any problems identified.

Examples by Industry

Retail

Cash handling, stockrooms, receiving areas, and the points where inventory goes missing.

Restaurants and Bars

Cash, liquor storage and access, closing routines, and patron safety.

Medical and Law Offices

After-hours access, access control procedures, storage of controlled or sensitive items, and protection of client and patient records.

Schools and Childcare

Entry control, visitor management, student check-in and check-out procedures, access procedures for service vendors, and perimeter fencing.

Houses of Worship and Nonprofits

Open-door settings, handling of collections and donations including back-office administration procedures, and events.

How It Works

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an audit take?

It depends on the size and complexity of your site. I give you an estimate during your consultation, before you decide.

How much does it cost?

The fee depends on the scope of the security audit. I discuss the cost with you while we develop your security audit proposal, before you decide.

Will my staff know?

That is your decision. Some parts of an audit work best when staff are not told in advance, so I agree on the approach with ownership or management beforehand.

Do you recommend specific products?

I recommend what you need, not specific brands. I sell no equipment and earn nothing from any product, so my advice stays independent.

Can you audit more than one location?

Yes. We discuss the scope together during your consultation.

Is a security audit a one-time project?

It can be. You can order a security audit as a one-off project or include it in an ongoing Security Director on Demand contract.

Prefer to Start with One Project?

Absolutely. Each of my services is available individually or as part of a Security Director on Demand contract. Many clients pair an audit with emergency procedures, threat assessment, and staff training. See my full list of services.

Start with a Confidential Conversation.

The first consultation is complimentary for new clients, and every consultation is confidential. I do not sell guards, alarms, or equipment, so you get independent advice. You will hear back directly within two business days.
