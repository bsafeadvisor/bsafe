Independent Advice, with Nothing to Sell

I do not sell security guards, alarms, cameras, or any other products or equipment. I only provide my services, so my findings are never tied to something I am selling. My only interest is giving you an honest assessment and recommendations that fit your needs and budget.

What the Security Audit Covers

A security audit is also called a physical security assessment or survey.

Your Space

  • Entrances, lighting, sight lines, access control, and how people move through the building or property
  • Current security arrangements, including CCTV, locks, alarms, and the use of security contractors
  • Surrounding areas, including parking lots and adjacent spaces

Your Procedures

  • Your current procedures and whether they are effective
  • Whether your staff and security contractors follow them

High-Risk Areas

  • How cash and sensitive information are handled
  • How high-value or easily stolen inventory is stored and accessed
  • Receiving areas
  • Sensitive areas, including financial management and human resources spaces

Testing

With your written authorization, security audits can also include testing of your physical security systems and processes, such as attempts to gain access to limited-access areas or determining whether staff and security contractors are following established procedures.

What You Receive

A Written Report

The report includes photographs and ranks each finding by assessed level of risk, so you know your biggest risks and vulnerabilities. It also includes my recommendations for improvement and proposals on how to address any concerns.

After the Report

Your report is confidential and delivered only to you, and I will sign a nondisclosure agreement if you ask. I can brief your management, help carry out the recommendations, and check the fixes later, either as a one-time project or through a Security Director on Demand contract.

Already Have a Guard Company or Alarm Vendor?

I can audit your security contracts and vendors to verify whether they are providing services in accordance with your contracts. I report the results to you, and if you choose, I can engage with the security contractor or vendor directly to address any problems identified.

Examples by Industry

Retail

Cash handling, stockrooms, receiving areas, and the points where inventory goes missing.

Restaurants and Bars

Cash, liquor storage and access, closing routines, and patron safety.

Medical and Law Offices

After-hours access, access control procedures, storage of controlled or sensitive items, and protection of client and patient records.

Schools and Childcare

Entry control, visitor management, student check-in and check-out procedures, access procedures for service vendors, and perimeter fencing.

Houses of Worship and Nonprofits

Open-door settings, handling of collections and donations including back-office administration procedures, and security arrangements for religious services or events.

Professional and Financial Offices

Access control, visitor and delivery procedures, protection of financial, personnel, and client records, and after-hours security.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a security audit take?

It depends on the size and complexity of your site. I give you an estimate during your consultation, before you decide.

How much does it cost?

The fee depends on the scope of the security audit. There is no charge for the proposal, and I discuss the cost with you while I develop it, before you decide.

Will my staff know?

That is your decision. Some parts of a security audit work best when staff are not told in advance, so I agree on the approach with ownership or management beforehand.

Do you recommend specific products?

I recommend what you need, not specific brands. I receive no referral fees or commissions from any product or vendor.

Can you audit more than one location?

Yes. I discuss the scope with you during your consultation.

Is a security audit a one-time project?

It can be. You can order a security audit as a one-off project or include it in an ongoing Security Director on Demand contract.

Prefer to Start with One Project?

Each of my services is available individually or as part of a Security Director on Demand contract. A security audit can often lead to or also require emergency procedures, threat assessment, and staff training. See my full list of services.
