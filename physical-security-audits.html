Physical Security Audits

A review of your space, your procedures, and your high-risk areas, followed by a written report of issues and recommendations.

Who it is for

Any business or organization with a physical space, cash, valuable inventory, or sensitive information.

What the audit covers

  • Your space. Entrances, lighting, locks, sight lines, access control, and how people move through the building.
  • Your procedures. Whether your staff follow the procedures you already have.
  • High-risk areas. Cash, high-value inventory, and sensitive information.

What you receive

A written report of issues and deficiencies, with recommendations for improvement. Audits can also include security penetration testing, assessment of current security arrangements, and audits of any security contracts or vendors.

Examples by industry

  • Retail. Cash handling, stockrooms, and the points where inventory goes missing.
  • Restaurants and bars. Cash, closing routines, and patron safety.
  • Medical and law offices. After-hours access and protection of client and patient records.
  • Schools and childcare. Entry control and visitor management.
  • Houses of worship and nonprofits. Open-door settings, collections, and events.

Related services

Many clients pair an audit with emergency procedures and staff training.
