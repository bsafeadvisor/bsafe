Security leadership from someone who has done the work.

B Safe Advisor Group is just me. When you call, you reach me, and I do the work myself.

I started B Safe Advisor Group to give small and medium-sized organizations and families the kind of security leadership that large employers take for granted.

My career has been in public safety. I was a full-time paramedic from 2004 to 2008, a firefighter and paramedic from 2005 to 2007, and a police officer from 2007 to 2008. I then spent 17 years as a federal criminal investigator and special agent with the U.S. Department of State's Diplomatic Security Service and the Offices of Inspector General at the State Department and USAID, including nine years as a supervisory criminal investigator (Assistant Special Agent in Charge). I investigated fraud, public corruption, violent crime, and employee misconduct, and I led teams of investigators.

From February 2025 to May 2026 I served as Acting Special Agent in Charge for all USAID Office of Inspector General investigations worldwide, overseeing the office's investigative staff and its active criminal investigations.

I also worked overseas at the U.S. Embassy in Islamabad, Pakistan and the U.S. Consulate General in Guangzhou, China, two postings with serious terrorism and counterintelligence concerns. There I worked on security, emergency planning, and threat mitigation for people and facilities, including oversight of multimillion-dollar armed guard force contracts.

What that experience taught me is that security is a balance. Every organization has a budget, a mission, and people who have to do their jobs. My approach is to work with everyone affected and build protections that are affordable and that people will follow.
