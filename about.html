Security leadership from someone who has done the work.

I started B Safe Advisor Group to give small and medium-sized organizations and families the kind of security leadership that large employers take for granted.

For more than 25 years, I have dedicated myself to keeping people and organizations safe. I began as a volunteer EMT in college and went on to serve as a paramedic, firefighter, and county law enforcement officer. I then spent 17 years as a federal special agent with the U.S. Department of State, including its Diplomatic Security Service and Office of Inspector General, and with the U.S. Agency for International Development Office of Inspector General (OIG). In these roles, I:

  • Protected U.S. embassies and consulates around the globe by managing security programs, guard forces, and emergency planning.
  • Provided executive protection and threat assessment and management for the Secretary of State, other senior government officials, and visiting foreign diplomats.
  • Led internal affairs and administrative investigations of allegations against law enforcement officers, senior government officials, and political appointees.
  • Served as the Sex Crimes Investigations program manager for the Department of State, where I led high-impact, victim-centric investigations and drafted the Department of State's worldwide policy on responding to allegations of sexual assault.
  • Managed up to 40 special agents investigating public corruption, contract fraud, grant fraud, and personnel misconduct, first as an Assistant Special Agent in Charge and then as Acting Special Agent in Charge.
  • Served as a criminal investigator and task force officer on the FBI Boston Joint Terrorism Task Force, where I led and took part in threat assessments, terrorism investigations, and responses.

From February 2025 to May 2026 I served as Acting Special Agent in Charge for all USAID Office of Inspector General investigations worldwide, overseeing the office's investigative staff and its active criminal investigations.

I also worked overseas at the U.S. Embassy in Islamabad, Pakistan and the U.S. Consulate General in Guangzhou, China, two postings with serious terrorism and counterintelligence concerns. There I worked on security, emergency planning, and threat mitigation for people and facilities, including oversight of multimillion-dollar armed guard force contracts.

What that experience taught me is that security is a balance. Every organization has a budget, a mission, and people who have to do their jobs. My approach is to work with everyone affected and build protections that are affordable and that people will follow.
