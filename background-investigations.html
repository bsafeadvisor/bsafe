Levels of Background Investigation

Whether you need a basic background check or a comprehensive investigation, I offer the service you need.

Level 1: Basic

  • Identity verification
  • Address verification
  • Criminal history check and civil court record check (nationwide)
  • Employment verification (up to two most recent employers)
  • Professional license check
  • Open-source and social media research
  • Watch-lists, sex offender registries, and similar registry checks

Level 2: Enhanced

Everything in Level 1, plus a pre-interview of the subject to develop additional leads and information to advance the background investigation.

Level 3: Full

Everything in Levels 1 and 2, plus:

  • Degree verification
  • Interviews of witnesses, both professional and personal, to verify personal and professional details and to identify any previously unknown misconduct

This level is well suited to vet executive and senior leader candidates.

Level 4: Comprehensive

Everything covered in Levels 1, 2, and 3, plus a federal-style background investigation:

  • Obtain and validate all personal details going back ten years
  • Verify all academic achievements
  • Analyze open-source records and proprietary data sources to develop a comprehensive set of facts about the subject

This level is best suited for non-employment matters such as political candidates, potential business partners, or as a part of business due diligence.

FCRA and Background Investigations

When the FCRA Applies

Background investigations that comply with the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) are mandatory when the results will be used for employment, volunteer positions, or appointments to organization boards.

Level 1, 2, and 3 background investigations can be completed as FCRA-compliant.

When It Does Not

Non-FCRA background investigations are most often used for risk management, fraud prevention, due diligence, political candidate vetting, or vendor screening.

Level 1, 2, and 3 background investigations can also be completed as non-FCRA.

Due to the extensive data sources needed to conduct a Level 4 background investigation, it can only be completed for non-FCRA purposes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which level and type of background investigation do I need?

It depends on the position and on whether the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) applies. A volunteer, a bookkeeper, and an executive do not carry the same risk, so I match the level of the investigation to the role. During the consultation, you and I decide which level and type of investigation best fits the position, meets your needs, and stays within your budget and timelines.

Who do you screen?

I screen candidates for employment and volunteer positions with organizations, potential business partners, political candidates, businesses and vendors, and household staff of families and individuals.

What will the subject be asked to do?

The subject signs a consent form, which may need to be notarized, and completes a biographical form. At Level 2 and above, I also interview the subject.

How long does a background investigation take?

The length of a background investigation depends on the type of investigation, the level of cooperation of the subject, and the complexity of the subject's background.

For example, Level 1 and Level 2 FCRA-compliant investigations are typically completed in five and seven business days, respectively. Alternatively, a Level 3 or 4 investigation takes at least three and up to four weeks to complete, depending on the circumstances.

Expedited turnaround times are available for an additional fee.

What happens if the check turns up something concerning?

I report the facts I verified, what I could not verify, and any discrepancies. I explain what they mean for the position. The decision is yours. If the FCRA applies, it sets steps you must follow before acting on a report.

Will my results stay confidential?

Yes. Your initial consultation, the subject's information, and my report remain confidential. I share findings only with you or as the law requires.

Can you screen subjects who live or worked outside Virginia?

Yes. Criminal and civil court record checks are nationwide, and I can verify employment and education wherever the subject lived or worked. Interviews can be conducted by phone or virtually.

How much does it cost?

The cost depends on the level and type of background investigation. I offer discounts for contracts of multiple background investigations, either simultaneously or over a specific period of time.

  • Level 1 FCRA-compliant background investigation: $250 per subject
  • Level 2 FCRA-compliant background investigation: $500 per subject
  • Level 3 FCRA-compliant background investigation: $1,750 per subject

The complexity and depth of a Level 4 investigation make the costs impossible to estimate without knowing the details of the proposed investigation. Please contact me directly to discuss costs for a Level 4 investigation. There is no charge for the initial consultation and the proposal.

Note: All costs provided above are estimates and may change based on your specific requirements and needs.
