Background Investigations

Pre-employment and pre-volunteer screening matched to the position, from high-risk roles to executive and fiduciary roles.

What it covers

I run the checks myself. Depending on what you need, the work ranges from FCRA-compliant checks to a deeper background investigation.

Consent and compliance

Background screening is regulated. I follow the Fair Credit Reporting Act where it applies, and the person being screened is asked for consent when the law requires it.

How a licensed investigator differs from an automated check

An automated check returns records. A licensed investigator also looks for what the records miss, checks that the person in the records is the person in front of you, and tells you what the findings mean for the position.

Who uses it

Employers, nonprofits, schools, childcare facilities, and houses of worship that hire staff or place volunteers.

The first consultation is complimentary for new clients, and every consultation is confidential. You will hear back directly within two business days.
