How a Licensed Investigator Differs from an Automated Check

An automated check returns records. A licensed investigator also looks for what the records miss, checks that the person in the records is the person in front of you, and tells you what the findings mean for the position.

Levels of Investigation

Whether you need a basic background check or a comprehensive investigation, I match the level to the position.

Level 1: Basic

A name check, data verification, and an extensive database search.

Level 2: Enhanced

Everything in Level 1, plus an interview of the candidate.

Level 3: Full

Everything in Level 2, plus interviews of witnesses. This level is well suited to executive and senior leader candidates.

Level 4: Comprehensive

A federal-style investigation. I obtain and validate all personal details going back ten years, verify all academic achievements, and analyze open source records and proprietary data sources to develop a comprehensive set of facts about the candidate. This level is best suited for non-employment matters such as political candidates.

FCRA and Background Investigations

Background investigations that comply with the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) are mandatory when the results will be used for employment purposes, tenant screening, or credit granting purposes. Under the FCRA, employment generally includes volunteer positions and appointments to company or organization boards.

Non-FCRA background investigations are most often used for risk management, fraud prevention, due diligence, or vendor screening. Any background investigation of a company is completed as a non-FCRA investigation.

Level 1, 2, and 3 background investigations can be completed as FCRA-compliant or not, depending on the purpose of the investigation. Due to the extensive data sources needed to conduct a Level 4 background investigation, it can only be completed for non-FCRA purposes.

I can help you choose the level and type of background screen that is best for your organization.

Consent and Compliance

Background screening is regulated. I follow the Fair Credit Reporting Act where it applies, and the person being screened is asked for consent when the law requires it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which level and type of background investigation do I need?

It depends on the position and on whether the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) applies. A volunteer, a bookkeeper, and an executive do not carry the same risk, so I match the level of the investigation to the role. During our consultation, we decide together which level and type of investigation best fits the position, meets your needs, and stays within your budget and timelines.

Do you screen volunteers as well as employees?

Yes, I screen candidates for employment and volunteer positions.

How long does a background investigation take?

A Level 1 FCRA-compliant investigation takes about five business days.

A Level 3 or 4 non-FCRA investigation takes at least two weeks and up to four weeks, depending on the circumstances.

Expedited turnaround times are available for an additional fee.

Do you screen household staff?

Yes. I screen household staff and current and future employees of families, as well as employees of organizations.

How much does it cost?

The cost depends on the level and type of background investigation. I offer discounts for contracts of multiple background investigations, either simultaneously or over a specific period of time. During our consultation, I will provide a full cost breakdown and the available options.
