Independent Advice, with Nothing to Sell

I do not sell security guards, alarms, cameras, or any other products or equipment. I only provide my services, so my findings are never tied to something I am selling. My only interest is giving you an honest picture of the person and the best client service.

What It Covers

I run the checks myself. Depending on what you need, the work ranges from checks that comply with the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) to a deeper background investigation.

Consent and Compliance

Background screening is regulated. I follow the Fair Credit Reporting Act where it applies, and the person being screened is asked for consent when the law requires it.

How a Licensed Investigator Differs from an Automated Check

An automated check returns records. A licensed investigator also looks for what the records miss, checks that the person in the records is the person in front of you, and tells you what the findings mean for the position.

Who Uses It

Employers, nonprofits, schools, childcare facilities, and houses of worship that hire staff or place volunteers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which type of check do I need?

It depends on the position. A volunteer, a bookkeeper, and an executive do not carry the same risk, so I match the depth of the screening to the role. You and I decide together during your consultation.

Do you screen volunteers as well as employees?

Yes. I screen both, and nonprofits, schools, childcare facilities, and houses of worship use these services for both.

How much does it cost?

The cost depends on the position and the depth of the screening. I give you the price during your consultation, before you decide.

Prefer to Start with One Project?

Each of my services is available individually or as part of a Security Director on Demand contract. Background investigations pair well with private investigations, skip tracing, and staff training. See my full list of services.

Start with a Confidential Conversation.

The first consultation is complimentary for new clients, and every consultation is confidential. I do not sell guards, alarms, or equipment, so you get independent advice. You will hear back directly within two business days.
