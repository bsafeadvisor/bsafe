Security in Arlington County

Arlington County is compact and dense, with office, residential, and nightlife corridors in Rosslyn, Clarendon, Ballston, and Crystal City. It is home to the Pentagon and Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport, and it sits directly across the Potomac River from Washington, D.C.

Security needs here are shaped by high foot traffic, shared buildings, late-night crowds, and frequent travel. Businesses need clear procedures for access, cash handling, and closing. Families and professionals benefit from travel security advice and from help when a threat or harassment concern arises.

Arlington County at a Glance

  • The Court House neighborhood hosts the county's administrative offices and courthouse and is treated as the county seat. Source for this fact about Arlington County
  • Arlington County had a population of 238,643 at the 2020 census. Source for this fact about Arlington County
  • At about 26 square miles, Arlington is the smallest self-governing county in the United States by area. Source for this fact about Arlington County
  • The Pentagon, headquarters of the U.S. Department of Defense, is in Arlington County. Source for this fact about Arlington County
  • Arlington National Cemetery, established in 1864, is the burial place of more than 400,000 members of the U.S. Armed Forces. Source for this fact about Arlington County
