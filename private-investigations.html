Frequently Asked Questions

What kinds of matters do you take?

I will consider any private investigation matter. Tell me what is happening during your consultation, and I will explain whether and how I can help.

Will my matter stay confidential?

Yes. I handle every matter in confidence, and I will sign a nondisclosure agreement if you ask.

How much does it cost?

The cost depends on the nature and scope of the work. A written contract sets out the scope and terms before I begin, so you know what to expect.

Can you work with my attorney or HR team?

Yes. I can work with your attorneys, HR teams, and others at your request to ensure the investigation meets your needs.

Prefer to Start with One Project?
