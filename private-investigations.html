Frequently Asked Questions

What kinds of matters do you investigate?

I will consider any private investigation matter. Tell me what is happening during your consultation, and I will explain whether and how I can help.

Will my matter stay confidential?

Yes. I handle every matter in confidence, and I will sign a nondisclosure agreement if you ask.

How much does it cost?

The cost depends on the nature and scope of the work. A written contract sets out the scope and terms before I begin, so you know what to expect.

Can you work with my attorney or HR team?

Yes. I can work with your attorneys, HR teams, and others at your request to ensure I investigate the specific areas of concern that matter to you.

Prefer to Start with One Project?

Each of my services is available individually or as part of a Security Director on Demand contract. Private investigations can often lead to or also require background investigations, skip tracing and missing persons, threat assessment, and physical security audits. See my full list of services.

Start with a Confidential Conversation.

The initial consultation is complimentary for new clients, and every consultation is confidential. I do not sell guards, alarms, or equipment, so you get independent advice. You will hear back directly within two business days.
