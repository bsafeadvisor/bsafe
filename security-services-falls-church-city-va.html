Security and Investigation Needs in the City of Falls Church

The City of Falls Church is compact and walkable, close to Metrorail, with a busy commercial corridor, a year-round farmers market, its own public schools, and many small businesses. That creates a wide range of needs, including:

  • Small businesses, shops, and restaurants: theft, payment fraud, cash handling, and employee conduct.
  • Professional, legal, and financial offices: confidential client information, fraud, and insider misconduct.
  • Schools, childcare facilities, and camps: emergency plans for threats, medical emergencies, and missing children.
  • Houses of worship and nonprofits: open-door gatherings, community events, volunteers, and donations.
  • Event organizers and venues: emergency planning and visitor safety.
  • Employees and families who travel: destination briefings and training for trips from Washington Dulles International Airport and Reagan National Airport.
  • Families and individuals: threats, harassment, home security, and scams that target residents.

A small footprint means one incident can affect an entire community, so clear emergency plans and trusted contacts matter. I help schools, businesses, and families build both.
