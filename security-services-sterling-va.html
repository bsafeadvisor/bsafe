Crime Trends in Sterling

Sterling is served by the Loudoun County Sheriff's Office Eastern Loudoun Station. Violent crime across Loudoun County fell 8% from 2024 to 2025, from 441 to 404 reports. Source: Virginia State Police NIBRS for violent crime counts In 2025, in the Eastern Loudoun Station, crimes against persons and property made up 41% of all instances reported to the Loudoun County Sheriff's Office. Theft represents about half of those crimes, with 863 larceny crimes, 99 auto thefts, and 35 burglaries, along with 321 destruction of property crimes. Source: Loudoun County Sheriff's Office crime dashboard, calendar year 2025 for these station statistics

Across Loudoun County, fraud-related crimes are up 6% from 2024 to 2025.

  • Forgery crimes are up 58%, from 135 to 213.
  • Impersonation crimes are up 26%, from 157 to 198.
  • Wire fraud crimes are up 13%, from 112 to 127.

Source: Virginia State Police NIBRS, calendar year 2025 compared with 2024 for these crime statistics
