Crime Trends in Sterling

Sterling is served by the Loudoun County Sheriff's Office Eastern Loudoun Station. In 2025, the station recorded 2,008 crimes against persons and property, 41% of the 4,866 recorded across the Sheriff's four patrol stations and more than any other station. Theft drives much of it, with 863 larceny offenses, 99 auto thefts, and 35 burglaries, along with 321 destruction of property offenses. Source: Loudoun County Sheriff's Office crime dashboard, calendar year 2025 for these station statistics

Across the whole Sheriff's jurisdiction, fraud-type offenses are up 9%, from 1,288 to 1,410. Forgery offenses are up 74%, impersonation offenses are up 30%, and credit card fraud offenses are up 17%. Source: Virginia State Police NIBRS, calendar year 2025 compared with 2024 for these crime statistics

The practical risks for Sterling businesses are theft, vandalism, and fraud.
