Security and Investigation Needs in Leesburg

Leesburg combines a historic downtown, busy retail centers, county government, a town airport, and growing neighborhoods. That mix creates a wide range of needs, including:

  • Retailers and restaurants: shoplifting and organized retail theft, employee theft, cash handling, and payment fraud.
  • Downtown bars, restaurants, and event venues: crowds, intoxicated patrons, fights, and emergency planning.
  • Law, finance, and professional offices: fraud, confidential matters, insider misconduct, and workplace threats.
  • Private schools, childcare facilities, and houses of worship: emergency plans for threats, medical emergencies, and missing children.
  • Property and business owners: theft, vandalism, inventory loss, and employee misconduct.
  • Families and individuals: threats, harassment, home security, and fraud, forgery, impersonation, and other scams.

Crime Trends in Leesburg

The Leesburg Police Department reported 2,413 offenses in 2025, down 5% from 2024. Shoplifting is the largest category, with 485 offenses, about one in five. Leesburg has about 11% of the county's population but accounted for 42% of the shoplifting offenses reported by the Sheriff's Office and the town police departments combined. Population: U.S. Census Bureau, July 2025 estimates for Leesburg and Loudoun County

Fraud-type offenses fell 5% overall, from 439 to 417, but some schemes are growing. Wire fraud offenses are up 56%, from 55 to 86. Forgery offenses are up 36%, from 59 to 80, and impersonation offenses are up 25%, from 40 to 50. Source: Virginia State Police NIBRS, calendar year 2025 compared with 2024 for these crime statistics

The practical risks for Leesburg businesses are financial fraud and retail theft, such as shoplifting, forged checks, wire fraud, and impersonation scams.

As a Certified Fraud Examiner and a member of the Mid-Atlantic Organized Retail Crime Alliance, I help retailers, restaurants, and offices spot theft and fraud, tighten the controls that stop them, and investigate when a loss occurs.
