Security and Investigation Needs in Leesburg

Leesburg combines a historic downtown, busy retail centers, county government, and growing neighborhoods. A 2023 market report describes Leesburg as home to tech, finance, and retail companies, with about ten million square feet of commercial space (Source). That mix creates a wide range of needs, including:

  • Retailers and shops: shoplifting and organized retail theft, employee theft, cash handling, and payment fraud.
  • Downtown bars, restaurants, and event venues: crowds, intoxicated patrons, fights, and emergency planning.
  • Law, finance, and professional offices: fraud, confidential matters, insider threats, and workplace threats.
  • Private schools, childcare facilities, and houses of worship: emergency plans for threats, medical emergencies, and missing children.
  • Property and business owners: theft, vandalism, inventory loss, and employee misconduct.
  • Families and individuals: threats, harassment, home security, and fraud, forgery, impersonation, and other scams.

Retail theft is a leading concern for businesses, and the courthouse area brings attorneys, clients, and heavy foot traffic downtown. I help stores, restaurants, and professional offices tighten their controls and investigate losses.

Crime Trends in Leesburg

The Leesburg Police Department reported fewer burglaries in 2025 than in 2024, from 38 to 16, and about 6% more larceny crimes, from 727 to 769. Violent crime reported to the department fell 6% over the same period, from 126 to 119 reports. Source: Virginia State Police NIBRS for violent crime counts Shoplifting is the most frequent type of crime report to Leesburg Police. Due to the substantial amount of retail establishments, shoplifting in Leesburg represented 42% of all shoplifting reports in Loudoun County.

Fraud crimes in Leesburg fell 11% from 2024 to 2025, from 380 to 337, but some fraud schemes are growing.

  • Wire fraud crimes are up 56%, from 55 to 86.
  • Forgery crimes are up 36%, from 59 to 80.
  • Impersonation crimes are up 25%, from 40 to 50.

Source: Virginia State Police NIBRS, calendar year 2025 compared with 2024 for these crime statistics

The practical risks for Leesburg businesses are financial fraud and retail theft, such as shoplifting, forged checks, wire fraud, and impersonation scams.

As a Certified Fraud Examiner and a member of the Mid-Atlantic Organized Retail Crime Alliance, I help retailers, restaurants, and offices spot theft and fraud, tighten the controls that stop them, and investigate when a loss occurs.
