B Safe at College

A six-hour safety course for students preparing to live away from home.

I am Zach Baumgart, a registered paramedic and former federal special agent with more than 25 years in public safety. I teach the course myself. About me

For Parents and Grandparents

Most students attend because a parent or grandparent signs them up. This course prepares your student for the safety decisions that come with living away from home, at college, trade school, or elsewhere.

For Students

Short version: you will leave knowing how to protect yourself, your belongings, and your friends once you are on your own.

The Course

A six-hour course for high school juniors, seniors, and graduates who are preparing to live away from home for the first time. Topics include fire safety, road safety, apartment and dorm security and safety, nighttime safety including alcohol and drug awareness, Active Threat Response (commonly referred to as active shooter or armed intruder training), travel safety, and other personal safety topics.

  • Who: high school juniors, seniors, and graduates preparing to live away from home
  • Length: six hours

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this course for?

High school juniors, seniors, and graduates who are preparing to live away from home. Most students attend because a parent or grandparent signs them up.

Is this only for students going to college?

No. The course is for students preparing to live away from home, whether at college, trade school, or elsewhere.

How do I register?

A parent or guardian registers and consents for any student under 18. Registration terms will be added when sessions are scheduled.

Can I book this course for a school, team, or other group?

Yes. I can teach B Safe at College to your group as a set course, and I can also build a custom course for your needs. See my training page, then send me a message or call or text me to start with a complimentary, confidential initial consultation.

Where is the course taught?
