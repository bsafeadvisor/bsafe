B Safe at College

A 5- to 7-hour safety course for students preparing to live on their own.

For parents and grandparents

Most students attend because a parent or grandparent signs them up. This course prepares your student for the safety decisions that come with living away from home, at college, trade school, or elsewhere.

The course

  • Who: high school juniors and seniors preparing to live on their own
  • Length: 5 to 7 hours
  • Where: to be decided

For students

Short version: you will leave knowing how to protect yourself, your belongings, and your friends once you are on your own.

Dates, times, and locations

Session dates, times, and locations will be posted here soon. To hear first, send me a message and choose Training.

Registration

A parent or guardian registers and consents for any student under 18. Registration terms will be added when the sessions are scheduled.
