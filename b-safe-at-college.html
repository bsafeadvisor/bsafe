The Course

A six-hour course covering the most common security and safety issues that lead to trouble and risky situations for students living away from home. Among the topics covered are:

  • Fire safety
  • Road safety
  • Apartment and dorm security and safety
  • Nightlife safety, including alcohol and drug awareness (opioids and Narcan)
  • Awareness and warning signs of drug-facilitated sexual assault, commonly referred to as “date rape” drugs
  • Medical emergencies
  • Situational awareness
  • Active Threat Response (commonly referred to as active shooter or armed intruder training)
  • Travel safety
  • Other personal safety topics

The course is designed for students preparing to live away from home for the first time who are:

  • High school juniors and seniors
  • High school graduates, whether headed to college, trade school, or elsewhere

Length: six hours, mostly lecture with built-in discussions and real-world examples of what can happen to students living on their own.

Cost: approximately $125 per student for prescheduled sessions.

No. The course is for students preparing to live away from home, whether at college, trade school, or elsewhere.
