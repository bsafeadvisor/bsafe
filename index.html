The problem

Most small and medium-sized organizations and many families face the same risks as large institutions: theft, fraud, threats, and emergencies that no one has a plan to handle. Few have access to professional security advice or a full-time security director. That leaves owners, administrators, and families making security decisions alone, or putting them off until something goes wrong.

Security Director on Demand

Security Director on Demand, also known as a fractional Security Director, gives you direct access to an experienced security leader when you need one, without the salary and overhead of an employee. You get one person who knows your organization, answers your questions, and steps in when something happens.

Security Director on Demand is also available to families and individuals.

What B Safe Advisor Group does

Assess and plan

Physical security audits

A review of your space, your procedures, and your high-risk areas, such as cash, high-value inventory, and sensitive information, followed by a written report of issues and recommendations.

Emergency procedures

An audit of your current plans, new procedures where needed, and staff training and drills for active shooters, medical emergencies, fire, lockdown, evacuation, severe weather, and missing children.

Threat assessment and management

I assess threats and online harassment, then help you manage and reduce the risk.

Travel security

Destination-specific briefings that assess local risks for international travelers and set out practical measures to reduce them, tailored to your trip. Guidance also covers choosing destinations and the resources available once you arrive.

Investigate

Private investigations

Investigations of theft, fraud, employee misconduct, and other crimes or internal policy violations. Childcare facilities, schools, and houses of worship also use these services for allegations of staff misconduct, and houses of worship use them for lost donations and collection plate irregularities. Families use them for crimes against family members, stolen property, and harassment and threats, including online.

Background investigations

Pre-employment and pre-volunteer screening matched to the position, from high-risk roles to executive and fiduciary roles.

Missing persons and skip tracing

Locating people to recover money or property.

Train

Training for your team

Training proposals come with my audit reports and emergency plans, and I offer annual or as-needed sessions for your team.

B Safe at Spring Break and B Safe at College

Courses that prepare teens and young adults for life away from home.

Custom training

Training built around your people, your space, and your risks, delivered on-site. Typical topics include:

  • Emergency medical response
  • Active shooter and active threat response
  • Workplace violence prevention and recognition
  • Fraud awareness and prevention
  • Retail theft and organized retail crime awareness
  • Online harassment and personal safety for families
