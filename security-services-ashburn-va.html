Crime Trends in Ashburn

Ashburn is unincorporated and served by the Loudoun County Sheriff's Office Ashburn Station. In 2025, crimes against persons and property at the station made up 28% of the 4,866 recorded across the Sheriff's four patrol stations, second only to Eastern Loudoun. Larceny was the largest property category, with 485 offenses, along with 234 destruction of property offenses, 65 auto thefts, and 16 burglaries. Simple assault accounted for most crimes against persons, with 472 offenses. Source: Loudoun County Sheriff's Office crime dashboard, calendar year 2025 for these station statistics

Across the whole Sheriff's jurisdiction, fraud-type offenses are up 9%, from 1,288 to 1,410. Forgery offenses are up 74%, impersonation offenses are up 30%, and credit card fraud offenses are up 17%. Source: Virginia State Police NIBRS, calendar year 2025 compared with 2024 for these crime statistics

The practical risk for most Ashburn residents and businesses is financial fraud and identity crime, such as forged checks, credit card fraud, identity theft, impersonation scams, and account takeovers.

As a Certified Fraud Examiner and former federal criminal investigator, I help organizations and families spot these schemes, tighten the controls that stop them, and investigate when a fraud scheme succeeds.
