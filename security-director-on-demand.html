Security Director on Demand

Also known as a fractional security director, Security Director on Demand gives you ongoing access to an experienced security leader, without the salary and overhead of a full-time hire.

What you get

  • Direct access. Call or text me when a question or concern comes up, and you reach me.
  • A leader who knows your organization. I learn your people, your space, and your risks, so my advice fits how you operate.
  • Help when something happens. I step in during an incident and help you decide what to do next.
  • Steady improvement. I review your procedures, plans, vendors, and security contracts, and I arrange audits and training as you need them.

These are examples, not a fixed list. The scope and my availability are set in a written agreement.

How it compares with a full-time hire

A full-time security director brings salary, benefits, and overhead, and most small and medium-sized organizations and families do not need that every day. With Security Director on Demand, you draw on senior experience when you need it, and you keep one person who already knows your situation.

Who it suits

  • Small and medium-sized organizations, including medical and law offices, professional services, retail, restaurants, and bars.
  • Private schools, childcare facilities, camps, and children's activity venues.
  • Nonprofits and houses of worship.
  • Families and individuals.

How it works

  • We start with a complimentary, confidential first consultation by phone or virtual meeting, about 15 minutes.
  • I recommend a scope that fits your needs.
  • A written agreement sets out the scope, availability, and terms, and you decide whether to proceed.
